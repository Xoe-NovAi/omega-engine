# 🔱 Biography: John Carmack — Career Arc Timeline
**Study Type**: Chronological Analysis with Engineering Decision Mapping
**Sovereignty Score**: 8/10 (Cross-referenced public records, interviews, code archives)

---

## 🌌 Overview

This timeline maps Carmack's career not by product releases but by **engineering philosophy shifts**. The same man who wrote the Wolf3D BSP renderer in 1991 was a very different engineer by the time he left Oculus in 2019. This document maps that evolution.

---

## 📅 Phase 1: The Prodigy (1984–1991)
**Age**: 14–21
**Key Theme**: Self-taught foundation, first-principles hacking

| Year | Milestone | Engineering Decision | Significance |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **1984** | First computer (Apple II) | Learns to program by writing text adventures | Self-taught methodology begins |
| **1987** | First game job (Softdisk) | Writes 6+ games in parallel for monthly magazine deadlines | Extreme productivity under constraints |
| **1990** | Co-founds id Software | Rejects publisher model for shareware distribution | First major "Engine-Data Separation" decision |

**Philosophy State**: "I can build anything if I understand the underlying math."

---

## 📅 Phase 2: The Engine Architect (1991–1996)
**Age**: 21–26
**Key Theme**: Inventing real-time 3D, building the id Tech stack

| Year | Milestone | Engineering Decision | Significance |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **1991** | Wolf3D development | Carmack's Expand + raycasting | First taste of 3D; performance-critical compression |
| **1993** | DOOM | BSP trees + visplanes + sector-based renderer | The canonical "Right Approximation" — fake 3D that looked real |
| **1994** | DOOM II | Engine reuse without rewrite | Carmack's Law of Consolidation in action |
| **1996** | Quake | True 3D engine + 3-month Pentium optimization | The most intense optimization blitz of his career |

**Philosophy Evolution**: "I can build a 3D engine" → "I can make a 3D engine *fast* on *any* hardware."

---

## 📅 Phase 3: The Polymath Emerges (1997–2003)
**Age**: 26–33
**Key Theme**: Expanding beyond games, rocket science, consolidation

| Year | Milestone | Engineering Decision | Significance |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **1997** | Quake II | Incremental engine upgrade, focus on networking | First major networking protocol (netchan) |
| **1999** | Quake 3 Arena | Puristic 3D engine, dedicated server focus | The pinnacle of the "thin wrapper" approach |
| **2000** | Armadillo Aerospace founded | Applies game engine physics to real-world rocketry | First major cross-domain application of first principles |
| **2002** | Doom 3 announced | Abandons "fake 3D" entirely for stencil shadows + per-pixel lighting | Carmack's Reverse: inverts a standard graphics technique |
| **2004** | Doom 3 released | Full 3D engine + mega-texture technology | The engine was 5 years ahead of hardware |

**Philosophy Evolution**: "I can build anything" → "I can build anything, and I should share *how*."

---

## 📅 Phase 4: The Industry Leader (2004–2013)
**Age**: 33–42
**Key Theme**: Open source advocacy, .plan culture peak, Rocketry peak

| Year | Milestone | Engineering Decision | Significance |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **2005** | Open source advocacy begins | Releases DOOM source code | First major open source release by a game company |
| **2008** | Armadillo wins X-Prize Cup Level 1 | Software-defined rocket guidance beats million-dollar hardware | Carmack's "software compensates for hardware" principle validated |
| **2011** | id Tech 5 + Rage development | Mega-texture streaming, 60fps target on consoles | First major console-centric engine (philosophy shift) |
| **2011** | Doom 3 BFG Edition | Carmack's Reverse optimized for modern hardware | Technical debt cleanup |
| **2012** | Joins Oculus as CTO | Leaves id Software (acquired by ZeniMax) | Biggest career pivot since founding id |

**Philosophy Evolution**: "I should share knowledge" → "I should spend my time on the *biggest* unsolved problems."

---

## 📅 Phase 5: The VR Pioneer (2013–2019)
**Age**: 42–48
**Key Theme**: Latency reduction, mobile VR, the asynchronous timewarp revolution

| Year | Milestone | Engineering Decision | Significance |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **2013** | Oculus CTO | Shifts from games to VR full-time | The Polymath applies to human perception |
| **2014** | Facebook acquisition | Carmack stays as CTO, arguing VR needs massive investment | Pragmatic decision: takes the money to solve the problem |
| **2015** | Samsung Gear VR (Note 4) | Carmack personally wrote the mobile VR renderer | Single engineer, 3 months, mobile VR that worked |
| **2016** | Asynchronous Timewarp | Solved motion-to-photon latency on mobile GPUs | The "Right Approximation" applied to human vision |
| **2017** | Oculus Go standalone VR | Carmack optimized the entire graphics pipeline for Qualcomm SoCs | "3 months on the Pentium" pattern repeated for mobile |
| **2018** | Oculus Quest | 6DOF tracking in a standalone headset | The culmination of 5 years of VR engineering |
| **2019** | Leaves Oculus | "I was no longer having fun" | Honest self-assessment |

**Philosophy Evolution**: "Hardware constraints can be solved by software" → "Human perception constraints are the *new* hardware constraints."

---

## 📅 Phase 6: The AGI Seeker (2019–Present)
**Age**: 48–55
**Key Theme**: Artificial General Intelligence, consulting, reflection

| Year | Milestone | Engineering Decision | Significance |
|------|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| **2019** | "I think" blog launched | Publishes detailed technical posts on AGI | Return to .plan culture in blog form |
| **2020** | Joins Keen Technologies | Focuses on AGI research | The Polymath enters the final frontier |
| **2021** | "I think" AGI posts | Explores neural architecture, agent systems, and consciousness | First-principles applied to intelligence itself |
| **2022-2024** | Consulting + public commentary | Advocates for AI safety, open research, empirical AGI | The mentor phase begins |
| **2025-2026** | Ongoing AGI research | Continues writing, thinking, and building | The arc is not yet complete |

**Philosophy Evolution**: "I can build a game engine" → "I can build a rocket" → "I can build VR" → "I can help build intelligence."

---

## 🔗 The Unifying Thread

Across all six phases, the same cognitive patterns persist:

1. **First-Principles Analysis**: Whether it's a BSP tree, a rocket engine, a VR headset, or neural architecture, he starts from the physics/mathematics, not from what others have done.
2. **The Right Approximation**: He consistently trades precision for performance, correctness for latency, and ideal solutions for working ones.
3. **Radical Transparency**: From .plan files to "I think" blog posts, he shares his current understanding as it evolves.
4. **The Polymath's Discipline**: He doesn't dabble. When he enters a new domain, he goes all-in for years at a time (rocketry: 8 years, VR: 6 years, AGI: ongoing).
