## §0 Executive Summary

The Master Synthesis era table starts at "Era 0 — Mar 2025, Lilith Tarot genesis." **That is correct but shallow.** Digging into `/media/arcana-novai/omega_vault/ANCESTRAL_HUB/origins/heart_of_omega/` and the recovered session `ses_1e18` reveals the true strata:

1. **The vision predates all technology.** It begins with the Architect's spiritual rebirth experience at age 36, his work with the archetypes Isis and Lilith, and a vow of gratitude that took the form of a custom Tarot deck.
2. **The moment of reckoning #1** (~spring 2025): He searched for an existing AI tool capable of building the deck companion to "divine standards," found none, and — *"brand new to local AI, and having no idea what I was getting into"* — decided to build his own. In his own words: *"Oh ya, I am not a programmer lol."*
3. **The moment of reckoning #2** (May 12-13, 2026): After 14 months and 6 eras, he stepped away, thought, and realized he had *"drifted off course from my original vision."* The result was the clean reclamation: wipe 33K files, keep only the proven patterns, and spend months of pure dev learning an entirely new field (async Python, MCP, agent architectures) before rebuilding.
4. **The core thesis has been stable since the first articulation**: local, sovereign AI owned by the user, running on mid-grade hardware, giving power back to the people. Everything else — WADs, Mandates, Hivemind, provider fabric — is implementation archaeology around that unchanging core.

## §3 The Two Reckonings (Answering Dispatch Q4)

### Reckoning #1 — "I am not a programmer" (Spring 2025)
> *"I soon came to realize that there was no AI tool out there at the level I required to create this offering to the divine standards I felt it required. So I, quite naively, decided to build my own lol. I first searched for the tool I needed, thinking that surely the local AI community had already built it. I was shocked to find otherwise. So, brand new to local AI, and having no idea what I was getting into, I set out to build this tool, not only for myself, but for the entire community. Oh ya, I am not a programmer lol."*

This is the genesis moment of the entire engine. The gap between vision (a deck worthy of the divine) and capability (no programming knowledge) was bridged not by shrinking the vision but by committing to ~8,000 hours of learning-by-building. The Mind Model Master Protocols (same period) show him immediately thinking in systems: constraints as design drivers, agents as autonomous components, phased roadmaps.

### Reckoning #2 — The Drift Realized (May 12-13, 2026)
> *"I have done much work within OpenCode since we last talked, then I took a step away and had some time to think. I have realized how far I have drifted off course from my original vision."*

After 14 months across 6 eras, 33K files, and the Temple-Grade over-engineering spiral (26 spheres, 108 Gates, 42 ideals enforced at runtime), the Architect stepped back and saw the cathedral had swallowed the chapel. The Path B synthesis captured the verdict: **"The user built a cathedral of ideas with a toolshed of code."** His response was the clean reclamation — delete everything, keep only the 5 proven design patterns, and accept months of pure development learning an entirely new field (AnyIO async, MCP protocol, OpenCode agent framework) before rebuilding. Git history confirms: the current repo begins 2026-05-22 with hardening sprints, not feature work. Foundation before decoration.
