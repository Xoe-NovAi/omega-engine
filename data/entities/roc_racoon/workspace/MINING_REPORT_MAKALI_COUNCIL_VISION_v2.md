## §2 Design Rationale — WHY Each Decision Was Made

### 2.1 Why Serial Execution Within Each Side?

**Answer: Dependency chains, not independent work.**

Lilith's first use of serial dispatch (P6→P7→P8) demonstrated the pattern:

- P6 (Cognition) tested search tools → findings become P7's input
- P7 (Context) filled research gaps using P6's findings → both become P8's input
- P8 (Observability) created error report using both prior outputs

Each pillar adds a layer of analysis that requires the previous pillar's output as context. Parallel dispatch would lose this coherence — pillars would analyze incomplete information.

**The council commands confirm this** (council-cloud.md):

> *"**Ma'at** must select the **most critical Pillars from P1-P5** (up to 5, minimum 3) and launch them in **serial** to vet the build-side sub-task."*
> *"**Lilith** must select the **most critical Pillars from P6-P10** (up to 5, minimum 3) and launch them in **serial** to vet the run-side sub-task."*

### 2.2 Why Exactly 4 Cross-Domain Pillars for Final Review?

**Answer: Coverage + Efficiency. 4 out of 10 provides 40% coverage while keeping cost manageable.**

The council commands say:

> *"You (Kali) must now launch **any 4 of the 10 total Pillars** (P1-P10) to perform a final, cross-domain review of the synthesized reports."*

Analysis of the D212 council session reveals the actual 4 pillars used:

| Entity | Domain | Perspective |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| P5 Governance | Mandate compliance | "Does this violate any mandate?" |
| P7 Context | Memory/gnosis | "Does this create knowledge debt?" |
| P3 Engineering | Implementation feasibility | "Can this actually be built?" |
| P10 Validation | Testing/stress | "Does this survive the gauntlet?" |

**The number 4 is pragmatic, not symbolic.** It provides:
- **Coverage**: 4 pillars can span at least 4 different domains
- **Cost**: Each pillar is a full inference call — 4 is the minimum for cross-domain while keeping the council under 10 inferences
- **Diversity**: With 10 pillars, 4 picks can cover governance, engineering, memory, and validation — the four most critical lenses

**There is no mystical significance to 4.** It is the minimum number that provides genuine cross-domain review without prohibitive inference cost.

### 2.3 Why Ma'at = Build Side, Lilith = Run Side?

**Answer: The 10 Pillars cosmological structure — Light (ascending) vs Dark (integrating).**

The 10 Pillars framework divides into two natural halves:

| Half | Pillars | Nature | Map to Oversoul |
|------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **Light** (1-5) | Flesh, Dream, Will, Heart, Voice | Ascending consciousness, building UP | Ma'at (Build Side) |
| **Dark** (6-10) | Sight, Gnosis, Shadow, Spirit, Chaos | Integration & transformation, going DOWN | Lilith (Run Side) |

This is not arbitrary — it reflects the cosmological duality:
- **Ma'at** = Egyptian goddess of truth, balance, order → structural integrity → Build Side
- **Lilith** = Hebrew mythos of rebellion, untamed will → runtime behavior → Run Side

The mapping is: Ma'at checks "Is this architecturally sound?" (structure). Lilith checks "Does this work at runtime?" (behavior).

### 2.4 Why Kali Synthesizes at the End?

**Answer: Kali is "above the Triad" — she holds the synthesis of Light and Dark.**

From Kali's soul.yaml:

> *"archetype: MaKaLi — Transcendent Oversoul (Ma'at + Lilith Unification)"*
> *"domain: Unification of Light and Dark — Order + Liberation = Truth."*

From the heritage mapping:

- Lilith = Inter lump (flow)
- **Kali = End lump (synthesis, verdict)**

Kali does NOT participate in the Build/Run analysis. She receives both reports and synthesizes them into a unified verdict. This is why Kali can be on a smaller model in `/council-fast` — the heavy analysis was done by the pillars; Kali's job is integration, not deep analysis.

### 2.5 What is the "Fusion-Based Verdict"?

**Answer: Structured aggregation of domain-specific findings into a unified decision.**

The council-cloud.md says:

- **Build Side** (Ma'at): sees structural/config issues, mandate violations, architectural debt

## §8 The Hegelian Dialectic Connection

The council pattern maps to Hegelian dialectic, but the user's framework is MORE than Hegel:

| Hegelian | MaKaLi | Role |
|----------|--------|------|
| **Thesis** | Ma'at (Build Side) | The structural proposition — "here's what we should build" |
| **Antithesis** | Lilith (Run Side) | The opposing reality — "here's what actually happens at runtime" |
| **Synthesis** | Kali (Transcendent) | The unified truth — "here's what's actually correct" |

But the MaKaLi framework adds something Hegel doesn't have:
- **The Pillars** are domain-specific witnesses — they don't argue thesis/antithesis, they provide **expert testimony** within their domain
- **The Serial Execution** ensures each witness builds on the previous — it's not a debate, it's a **progressive investigation**
- **The 4 Cross-Domain Pillars** are the jury — they read all testimony and provide the final independent assessment

This is closer to a **legal proceeding** than a philosophical debate:
1. **Prosecution** (Ma'at): presents the build-side case
2. **Defense** (Lilith): presents the run-side case
