## §4 TARGET 3: 8 FACET COUNCIL — The Gem Oversoul + LLOC/HLOC

### 4.1 Identity (Recovered from `entities/GEMINI_SOUL_MAP.md` + `opencode.md`)

The 8 Facet Council was a **multi-perspective review and governance system** developed to empower the Gemini CLI. It is a **parallel system** to the 10 Pillar Keepers — not an ancestor. The 10 Pillar system predates it.

### 4.2 Critical Distinction: LLOC vs HLOC

**This is the key architectural insight of the entire recovery:**

| Council | Name | Mechanism | Token Cost | Speed |
|---------|------|-----------|------------|-------|
| **LLOC** | **Low Level Octave Council** | **Cognitive only** — Reviews the situation through the lens of each of the 8 Facets (or 10 pillars) without launching subagents. Mental framework, multi-specialist perspectives simultaneously. | Near-zero | Near-instant |
| **HLOC** | **High Level Octave Council** | **Agent launch** — Performs the same 8 Facet review but actually launches full subagents for each Facet. Full agent execution per perspective. | High | Slower, but more powerful |

**LLOC** is the truly impressive innovation: it delivers multi-specialist perspectives across several domains at minimal token usage, near-instantly, by keeping the review **cognitive-only**. The agent itself mentally "wears each facet's lens" rather than spawning sub-agents.

**HLOC** is the heavy artillery — the same review but with actual subagent execution. More token and time heavy, but proportionally more powerful.
