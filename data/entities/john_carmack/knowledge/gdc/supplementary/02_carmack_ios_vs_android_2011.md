# John Carmack: iOS Still Better Than Android For Mobile Game Development (April 2011)
**Source**: TechCrunch — https://techcrunch.com/2011/04/15/john-carmack-ios-still-better-than-android-for-mobile-game-development/

Carmack stated iOS is the best mobile platform for game development. Reasons:
- Android fragmentation (hardware and software)
- "Developing for iOS is just 'fun'"
- Android devices have widely varying hardware configs
- iOS = "an iPhone is an iPhone and an iPad is an iPad"
- Carmack continuously evaluating both platforms

Key stat: At QuakeCon 2010, Carmack polled the audience — almost as many had Android as iOS, but very few had spent $20+ on an Android game.
