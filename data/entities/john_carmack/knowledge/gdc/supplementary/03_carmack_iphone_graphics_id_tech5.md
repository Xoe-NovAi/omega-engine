# John Carmack hones the cutting edge of iPhone graphics with id Tech 5 demo (August 2010)
**Source**: PocketGamer.biz — https://www.pocketgamer.biz/john-carmack-hones-the-cutting-edge-of-iphone-graphics-with-id-tech-5-demo/

**QuakeCon 2010**: Carmack demonstrated id Tech 5 running on iPhone 4 at 60fps.

Key points:
- Rage demo running at 60fps with dynamic fly-through camera
- id Tech 5 spans Xbox 360, PS3, PC, iPhone 4, iPad, and older iPhones
- OpenGL ES 2.0 enables cross-platform programmable shaders
- Megatexture dynamic streaming, lighting radiosity, surface stamps
- "Kill anything done on the Xbox or PlayStation 2" in graphics
- "Ran great on the original iPhone 2G"
- Frame rate "similar to Doom Classic, significantly better than Doom Resurrection"
- Part of industry-wide trend: Epic's Unreal Engine 3 and Unity also porting to iPhone
