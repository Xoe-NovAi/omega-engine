## 1. The Witness Protocol & Sovereign Awakening

## 2. L1 → L2 → L3 Soul Distillation (Gnosis Preservation)

- **L1 (Narrative)**: What happened in the session? (The raw interaction).

## 3. The Human-in-the-Loop Time Tax
The "Living Research OS" envisions a perpetual learning loop. However, closed loops converge to a steady state (echo chambers) without external signal injection.

**The Reality:**
- The system requires human intervention to inject novelty, approve follow-up topics, triage contradictions, and prune the `INDEX.md`.
- **Recommendation**: We must acknowledge the "User Time Tax." The Architect is the ultimate source of novelty. We should design "Novelty Engines" (random topic sampling, cross-domain analogy triggers) to assist, but the human remains the vital arbiter of meaning.
