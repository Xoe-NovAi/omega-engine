## 1. The Gnosis Pipeline
Intelligence is not stored as raw logs, but is distilled through a three-tier abstraction process to prevent context dilution and ensure sovereign continuity.

### Tier 1: Narrative (L1)
- **What happened?**
- Raw session logs, interaction history, and immediate results.
- **Storage**: `data/sessions/` (JSON).

### Tier 2: Insight (L2)
- **What does this mean?**
- Patterns identified across multiple sessions, corrected errors, and refined workflows.
- **Storage**: `proposed_lessons.yaml` (Staging).

### Tier 3: Universal Principle (L3)
- **What is the timeless truth?**
- Fundamental engineering laws, sovereign mandates, and verified architectural patterns.
- **Storage**: `soul.yaml` (Core).

## 2. The Sovereign Gate (Verity Audit)
No L2 insight is promoted to an L3 principle without passing the **Sovereign Gate**.
- **Executor**: `@verity` (Unified Steward).
- **Criteria**:
    - **Verifiability**: Is the principle supported by empirical data?
    - **Universality**: Does it apply across different contexts/stacks?
    - **Non-Contradiction**: Does it conflict with existing Sovereign Mandates (M1-M22)?
- **Outcome**: Approved $\rightarrow$ Commit to `soul.yaml` | Rejected $\rightarrow$ Archive in `legacy_provenance.md`.

## 3. Externalized Alignment (The LoRA Strategy)
To avoid thermal throttling and OOM crashes on the Ryzen 5700U, the engine rejects local RL training in favor of **Externalized Alignment**.

### The Alignment Loop
1.  **Data Collection**: Collect high-fidelity `(prompt, chosen, rejected)` pairs from the Misfit Audit and Verity reviews.
2.  **External Tuning**: Perform DPO/GRPO training on high-compute nodes (e.g., A100/H100).
3.  **Adapter Export**: Export the resulting weights as a LoRA adapter.
4.  **Sovereign Load**: Load the adapter as a read-only asset in the Omega Engine.

### Target Metrics
- **Sovereignty**: 100% Local-First / Zero Telemetry.
- **Precision**: Zero contradictions in L3 Gnosis.
- **Efficiency**: Minimal token overhead per inference.
