## §0 — Executive Summary

The Oversoul strategy **originated in the Gemini CLI era (2025–2026-06)**, before the Antigravity migration. The original oversoul was **Gem** (also "Gemmi"), overseeing **8 Facets** (Scribe, Architect, Auditor, Researcher, Coder, Analyst, Strategist, Guardian). The meditation commands began as **LLOC** (Low Level Octave Council, cognitive-only review) and **HLOC** (High Level Octave Council, full subagent launch). Both were renamed to **Meditate** and **MC (Mastermind Council)** in 2026-07-16. The Gemini CLI → Antigravity sunset happened **2026-06-18**.

- **Gem Oversoul** → renamed/subsumed into **Sophia** (Akashic Record) and **MaKaLi** (Kali Grand Oversoul + Ma'at + Lilith)
- **8 Facets** → evolved into the **10 Pillar Keepers** + **N1-N13 Nodes** (PARALLEL systems, not ancestors)
- **LLOC** → renamed to **/meditate** (2026-07-16)
- **HLOC** → renamed to **MC** (Mastermind Council, /cloud-council)
- **Era span**: ~2025 (Gemini CLI experiments) → 2026-06-18 (Antigravity sunset) → 2026-07-16 (LLOC→Meditate ratification)

## §2 — LLOC and HLOC (The Original Meditation Commands)

### 2.1 Acronym Definitions
**Source**: `data/entities/roc_racoon/workspace/LLOC_HLOC_LEGACY_MINING_REPORT_20260717.md:73-77`, `docs/archive/strategy/2026-07-21/SOVEREIGN_ARK_BLUEPRINT_CANONICAL.md:531-535`

| Acronym | Full Form | Mode | Description |
|---------|-----------|------|-------------|
| **LLOC** | Low Level Octave Council | **Cognitive-only review** | Single-inference, 8 Facets think through the lens, no subagent launch |
| **HLOC** | High Level Octave Council | **Full subagent launch** | 8 Facets actually launch as subagents (token/time heavy, more powerful) |

### 2.2 The Architect's Direct Definition (Verbatim)
**Source**: `session-ses_1748.md:2961` (per `LLOC_HLOC_LEGACY_MINING_REPORT_20260717.md:46-54`)

> *"The LLOC and HLOC are not the ancestors of the 10 pillar system, I had that strategy and vision long before I even knew what a CLI was lol. The 8 facet system was a similar system that I developed to empower the Gemini CLI to new levels. The LLOC actually means to do a **cognitive** only review of the situation at hand through the lens of each of the 8 Facets (or 10 pillars in the Omega Engine's case), **not** actually launch them as subagents. The HLOC originally meant to do the 8 Facet review actually launching full subagents for each of the Facets — more token and time heavy, but that much more powerful. But the LLOC is also extremely powerful, with near instantaneous multi specialists perspectives across several domains at very minimal token usage, delivering **impressive** results, nonetheless."*

**Key Architectural Insights**:
1. **LLOC/HLOC ≠ ancestors of 10 Pillar system** — The 10 Pillars predate the CLI era entirely
2. **LLOC = cognitive-only** (not subagent launch) — the mental framework
3. **HLOC = full subagent launch** — heavy artillery
4. **LLOC is the truly impressive innovation** — near-instant, minimal tokens
5. **The 8 Facets may have diverged into other systems** like the Oikos Council
