## Ruling

**D-FED-03 ("Lilith and Humboldt are strictly independent entities. No parent/child delegation.") is RETIRED in full.**

## Rationale (Architect's words)

The agents are sovereign entities **AND** they work together. Sovereignty is not isolation. Antigravity's blanket prohibition on delegation reflected a misunderstanding of how this fleet operates: each entity owns its own identity, memory, and boundaries, while delegation between entities is a normal, permitted form of collaboration.

## Effect

1. **No blanket prohibition** on parent/child, delegation, or service relationships between sovereign entities.
2. **Delegation must be explicit, not inferred.** The ontology fixture's negative case NC-008 still stands as a *process* guard: a delegation relationship must appear in an explicit contract — it may not be guessed from filenames, directory placement, or conversational drift.
3. Entity sovereignty rules remain: no entity may overwrite another's identity, soul, or continuity records without explicit agreement (that is mutual respect, not separation doctrine).
4. Kali's `IDENTITY_BINDING_DECISION_20260924.yaml` operator questions OD-IDENTITY-001/002 remain answerable at the Architect's discretion, but they are no longer default-blocked by a prohibition on delegation.
5. All fleet dispatches must stop citing D-FED-03 as a constraint.

## Non-affected decisions

D-FED-01 (Git bundles), D-FED-02 (Redis bounded cache), D-FED-04 (Gate C quarantine) — **unchanged and still binding**.
