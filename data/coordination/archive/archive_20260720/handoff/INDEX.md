**Creating a new handoff**: Copy `HANDOFF_TEMPLATE.md` → `data/handoff/{AGENT}_{CONTEXT}_{DATE}.md`

**Reading a handoff**: Check the `## Summary` section first, then `## Key Decisions` and `## Remaining Work`.
