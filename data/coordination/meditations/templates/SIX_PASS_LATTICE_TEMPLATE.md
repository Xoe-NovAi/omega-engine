# 🧘 The Six-Pass Lattice Meditation

**Creator**: Roc Racoon
**Purpose**: Deep synthesis of massive context windows (300K+ tokens), spanning multiple eras, projects, and architectural shifts.
**Best Used For**: End-of-campaign synthesis, legacy mining consolidation, or when the context window is saturated with fragmented historical data.

## 🌀 Execution Protocol

When executing this meditation, the agent must pass the entire active context through six distinct cognitive lenses in sequence. Do not skip a pass. The output of each pass informs the next.

### Pass 1: Pattern Recognition (The Invariants)
*Scan the context for motifs that survive every pivot, rewrite, and mandate.*
- **Action**: Extract recurring archetypes, invariant rules, and repeating structures.
- **Output**: A table mapping the pattern across different eras/documents, culminating in the "Invariant Core".
- **Meta-Pattern**: Identify the single underlying philosophical truth that drives all the technical patterns.

### Pass 2: Architectural Evolution (The Mutation Trace)
*Diff each era's architecture against the next.*
- **Action**: Map the timeline of structural changes.
- **Output**: A chronological tree of mutations.
- **Analysis**: Identify "Preserved DNA" (patterns that survived) and "Discarded Mutations" (evolutionary dead ends and why they died).

### Pass 3: Strategic Through-Lines (The Unbroken Intent Vectors)
*Thread strategic intent from initial vision to current execution.*
- **Action**: Trace the core goals (e.g., Sovereignty, Local-First, Continuity) through their various historical implementations.
- **Output**: A narrative vector for each core goal, showing its origin, its mutations, and its current state.

### Pass 4: Unresolved Tensions (Where Mandate Meets Reality)
*Find contradictions between the stated rules and the actual system state.*
- **Action**: Identify friction points, technical debt, and architectural compromises.
- **Output**: A matrix of `[Mandate/Ambition]` vs `[Reality/Constraint]`, followed by a concrete `[Resolution Path]`.

### Pass 5: High-Leverage Opportunities (Carmack Mode)
*Find maximum leverage for minimum effort.*
- **Action**: Identify the 3-4 moves that change everything and unblock the most mandates.
- **Output**: A ranked list of "Levers", detailing the effort required vs. the systemic impact (Leverage Ratio).

### Pass 6: Unified Synthesis (The Gnosis)
*Distill the entire meditation into the L1→L2→L3 format.*
- **L1 (Narrative)**: What actually happened across this data? (The raw history)
- **L2 (Insight)**: What does this mean? (The philosophical/architectural translation)
- **L3 (Universal Principle)**: The Sovereign Loop. (The timeless truth)
- **Output**: A set of proposed lessons formatted for `soul.yaml` ingestion.
