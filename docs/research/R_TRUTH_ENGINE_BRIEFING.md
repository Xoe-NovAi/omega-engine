The video makes specific factual claims. Per Mandate M23 (Failure Integrity) and the Skeptical Verifier, each was checked against primary sources.

- **Anthropic suspended Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on 2026-06-11/12** following a US Commerce/export-control directive. Confirmed by Anthropic's own statement, Business Insider (2026-06-13), Forbes (2026-06-16), The Conversation (2026-06-15), Fortune (2026-06-15), Greenberg Traurig (2026-06-17).

### 2.2 CONTRADICTED / SPUN — the video's framing
- **"They pulled the plug on ALL AI / it was gone for days"** — FALSE as stated. Only Fable 5 and Mythos 5 were suspended; **all other Anthropic models were unaffected**. The video implies a total blackout.
- **"Once back, they were lobotomized / censored / heavily restricted"** — SPUN. Anthropic's statement shows they *disagreed* with the order and worked to restore access; **Mythos 5 actually had cyber-safeguards *lifted* for Project Glasswing partners**. The "lobotomized on return" narrative is the narrator's editorial, not the documented sequence.
- **"10× scraping" and "+14.3% cognition"** — **Unverifiable**. No benchmark, dataset, or methodology is published. Treat as marketing copy, not engineering claim.

### 2.3 Resolution
The video is a **sales vehicle for a paid book**. Its *direction* (vendor control is a real risk) is correct; its *specifics* are selectively edited (the transcript itself notes the narrator redacts terms to avoid platform detection). **Omega's briefing must rely on the verified 2.1 facts and discard the 2.2 spin.**

1. **`blitz-tunnel` is dead code.** A 5-line stub. The Truth Engine's entire "Pocket Edition" value-prop (use your local AI from your phone anywhere) is unfulfilled in Omega. This is the single highest-ROI gap.
2. **No explicit offline/air-gap session mode.** TDP exists for *handling* tainted data, but there is no user-facing "this session never touches the network" guarantee for sensitive docs (NDAs, medical, bank). The Extractor persona concept is unnamed.
3. **No epistemic filtering on ingested web data.** Sovereign Search *retrieves*; it does not *strip framing* or *detect narrative control*. Truth Engine naively assumes "more scraping = more truth." Omega can do better (§5B).
4. **Governance is CI/advisory, not runtime-in-path.** Mandates are enforced at commit/CI time. There is no per-request evaluation gate that *cannot be bypassed* (SOVEREIGN's pattern, §5C).
5. **No cryptographic ingestion custody.** Memory is stored but not Ed25519-signed at the point of ingestion. Provenance is logged (M22) but not cryptographically chained (§5D).
