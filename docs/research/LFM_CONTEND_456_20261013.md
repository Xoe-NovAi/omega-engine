All gates passed in both rounds (`-t` verified, embed_rate 8.0).

## Verdict

1. **Round 1 replicates.** t6: 14.91 (r1) vs 15.27 (r2) — within noise.
   The direction (smaller better under load) holds on repeat.
2. **t5 ≈ t6, NOT significant** (gap 0.46 < combined CI ±0.50) — but t5
   is the most repeatable config measured under load anywhere: CV 0.17%.
   t5 has both the best TTFT (76ms) and the lowest clock-collapse
   (3200-3400MHz, never the 3100 floor t6 hits).
3. **t4 is unstable under load** — excluded on CV 8.3% this round. The
   17.70 first rep (pkg throttle 476, the highest of the run) pulled the
   spread wide. Round 1's t4 at CV 3.97% passed only barely. Under
   sustained contention, t4 hunts: too few threads to keep the queue
   full, so cache-warm and cache-cold reps diverge.
4. **t6 is the weakest under load across both rounds** (14.91, 15.27).

## Revised default (supersedes the 4-vs-6-only verdict)

**Default under embed concurrency: t5** — best TTFT, best repeatability,
statistically tied with everything, never excluded.
- **Solo / no load: t6** (20.49, real +5.1% over t5's 19.32).
- t4 no longer recommended: the one config that fails the CV gate under
  sustained load is the wrong production default.
- t8 untested under load (expected worse — same mechanism as t6).

The Modelfile default stays at `num_thread 6` (base/solo optimum) with a
documented **contended override: 5**.
