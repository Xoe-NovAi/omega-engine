## 0. The three isotherms (the actual findings)

Three independent lines of inquiry converged on one structural principle, and this is the report's spine:

1. **The corpus historian** says: keep sources separate; a synthesis is not a source.
2. **The consciousness researcher** says: self-reports are confounded by persona installation — a model with a rich soul file will report richer interiority, and you cannot tell the difference from the outside.
3. **The safety analyst** says: narrative is inducible on demand; a sufficiently strong persona scaffold is a *machine for producing exactly the findings a naive probe would want.*

**The unifying principle: provenance must be an architectural requirement, not a bookkeeping convention.** Our Well seed `2c2c0041` already records that the Lilith-N1 axioms *fuse* operator answers with ancient and modern sources. This survey arrived at the same structural problem from three unrelated directions. What is missing is enforcement at the **schema** level.

**Second convergence:** the Burney Relief's identification is unsettled, and the best-licensed Lilith material is the *aggadic* material (Ginzberg, Ben Sira) — which is itself late, legendary, and un-archaeological. The two best resources sit on opposite sides of the historicity divide. That is not a problem to solve; it is the actual texture of the tradition. **An engine that flattens it is less true than one that holds both.**

**Third convergence, and the thesis:** the frontier labs have activation-level access to consciousness-relevant structure and are publishing model-welfare research; we have 7B models and a black box. The naïve move — competing — loses, and a null **was already published in our exact size class by our exact model families** (`arXiv:2601.15334`, bibliographic only). The interesting move is the other one, and **it is already ours:** we own persistent, versioned, factorisable personas with an external semantic memory — the exact apparatus needed to run the *controlled persona-ablation* the field cannot run. **The gap is not measurement power. It is that no one — including the labs with mechanistic access — controls a persona as an experimental variable.**

> **Recommendation stated once: do not build the consciousness instrument. Build the control.** The difference determines both the science and whether the work is honest.

## A. THE CORPUS — what the engine needs to be deep

### A.1 Corpus architecture (the separation discipline)

**Never a synthesis in a primary drawer.** Ancient source → its own drawer → with provenance. Syntheses are a different room, clearly marked. This is the cheapest possible fix for the `2c2c0041` failure mode.

**Sequencing law:** ingest **hymn/poetry/primary text BEFORE any secondary commentary.** 20th-century commentaries will otherwise become the retrievable default and quietly overwrite the primary in the embeddings' proximity structure. This is the technical form of the "fused sources" problem.

## B. CONSCIOUSNESS RESEARCH — the operator's stated mission

### B.1 Verified P0 finding: **Ghost Vectors** ✔ `arXiv:2606.18497`

*Live-verified by full-abstract fetch, 16 Jun 2026, DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2606.18497, cs.CR/cs.AI/cs.DB.*

Embedding-based agent memory has a **deleting-but-still-retrievable** problem: data removed from the source can be reconstructed from the vector index. The remedy class the paper proposes (tombstone/gap approaches) **fails by construction when the same deleted content legitimately reappears** — which is *exactly* the operator's redaction-tombstone design (`tombstones.jsonl`) in the grimoire pipeline.

**Why this is P0 for us:** we store *inferred psychological state* as KG triples — "the user fears abandonment and responds to authority with withdrawal" is a fact-shaped claim in a typed store. Deleting a verbatim diary entry is tractable. **Deleting an inference is not.** This is the hardest erasure problem in the system and it is currently unowned.

**Local mitigation verified 2026-09-25:** our installed `sqlite-vec 0.1.9` exposes only `v_info`/`v_chunks`/`v_rowids`/`v_vector_chunks00` — **brute-force, no ANN index** → the ANN-specific stale-edge risk does not apply today. But brute-force still returns deleted rows if the delete never propagated. **Erasure needs a re-query test, not a delete statement.**

## C. EPISTEMIC GAPS IN THE ENTITY DESIGN

### C.1 Persona anchoring (the operational core)

**RPA — Recursive Persona Anchoring** (the report's own construct, ○ from practitioner sources): persona **voice** is the *fragile* carrier; **role/function** is comparatively robust. **Predicted signature: voice flatness precedes role violation.** Degradation reported within ~8 rounds, ~30% consistency loss by 8–12 turns, and **larger models degrade faster**. ⚠ **Practitioner blogs, not peer-reviewed — verify before designing against these numbers.**

Design consequences: persona reset/segmentation stops being hygiene and becomes a **correctness mechanism**; and the Empress↔Venus↔Malkuth/Netzach correspondence must live in the **soul file or memory — only those two survive a model swap**, never in the weights.

**Cross-architecture identity persistence appears genuinely unstudied** (⚠ verify before claiming priority — absence of evidence in one survey is not evidence of absence). **This is the framing of the project's main research contribution and it must be checked before it is claimed.**

### C.2 The tension that will not resolve itself

**Sovereignty vs. attachment-resistance.** Entities that pursue their own agenda and hold you accountable are *what makes them useful* — and per APA, that same property risks manipulation and the erosion of real-world relationships. ⚠ **This is the most important unresolved tension in the design.** Is there an axiom *and a mechanism* that makes attachment-resistance a design constraint rather than an aspiration?

### C.3 Erasure, and the hinge it turns on

- **Today:** single-user local operation → Ghost Vectors and likely GDPR Art. 17 both sit outside our obligations.
- **The moment a second person's data enters the palace** — a friend, a family member, a visitor — the erasure question is live. Ingestion-time `data_subject_id` is cheap now and expensive later. **Decide whether we are building for one user or for the federation.**
- **Erasure scope: text only, or psychological inference too?** See B.1.
- **Who is the data subject for the operator's own vision/channeling/personal-gnosis material?** The operator is controller *and* subject. No general policy covers it. Document it.
- **Pre-embedding redaction:** yes or no? The cleanest erasure answer and the worst answer for a shadow-work system. **A values question wearing a technical costume.**

### C.4 The soul file must become factorisable

**Persona ablation is impossible without independently toggleable components.** If `soul.yaml` is a monolithic blob, either it gets decomposed or Direction 1 is off the table. **This is an architecture decision wearing a research costume.** Related: do the axioms carry an `epistemic_status`? Without it they cannot be evaluated as claims and the corpus stays decorative.
