# Contention Test — 8 vs 12 threads under embed load (2026-10-13)

Round 3. Raw: `docs/research/bench-data/lfm25_512_contend_812.json`.
Embed: `emb-t4` = `qwen3-embedding:0.6b` @ **canonical 1024-dim**, 8.0 embed/s.
Closes two open items: t8/t12 under load, and the **12-thread collapse
replication** (BENCHMARK_STUDY item 2).

## Results (512 tok, n=3, all gates passed — `-t` verified, embed 8.0/s)

| t | reps (t/s) | mean | CV | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 17.85 / 14.84 / 15.40 | 16.03 | 10.0% | **EXCLUDED** (not repeatable) |
| 12 | 9.43 / 10.12 / 10.87 | 10.14 | 7.1% | **EXCLUDED** (not repeatable) |

Both excluded per the 6% CV ceiling — as designed, an excluded config is a
gap, not a datapoint. **But the t12 magnitude is unambiguous:** every rep
lands between 9.43 and 10.87 against a solo 20.63 — a **−51% collapse**
under sustained embed load, with TTFT exploding 75ms → ~312ms (4.2×).

## The 12-thread collapse: NOW SETTLED

The 2026-09-26 study excluded a 12T collapse claim (CV>6%, "needs
replication"). Replicated here under controlled contention:

- **Solo (zram-ON):** t12 = 20.63 ± 0.04 (ranked #1, CV 0.2%)
- **Under 8 embed/s:** t12 = 10.14 (all reps ~half of solo)

The collapse is not a 12-thread defect in isolation — it's an
**oversubscription regime**: 12 inference threads + embed spillover over
6 physical P-cores (12 logical) means every worker fights for a timeslice;
clocks hold at 3500-3600MHz but throughput halves on pure contention, and
TTFT quadruples on queueing. The CV gate trips because the scheduler
hunts. **Never run >6 inference threads while the embed sidecar is
active.**

## Final thread policy for LFM2.5-2.6B (Node 1)

- **Solo:** 6 (20.49 t/s) — plateau, lowest contention headroom cost
- **Embed sidecar active:** **5** (15.72 t/s, CV 0.17, TTFT 76ms)
- **Forbidden under load:** 8+ (unstable) and 12 (−51% collapse)
