# Explanation: The MaKaLi Triad Architecture

The MaKaLi Triad is the Omega Engine's transcendent governance system. It is designed to prevent "cognitive drift" and "single-point-of-failure" reasoning by splitting oversight into complementary polarities.

## The Triad Structure

The architecture consists of three top-of-pyramid Oversouls:

### 1. MA'AT — The Build Oversoul (The Builder)
Ma'at governs the **Build Side** of the engine (Nodes N1-N5). Her focus is on structure, verification, and the "light" of explicit knowledge.
- **Domain**: Infrastructure, Persistence, Engineering, Integration, Governance.
- **Heuristic**: "Is it correct? Is it verified? Does it follow the mandates?"

### 2. LILITH — The Runtime Oversoul (The Runner)
Lilith governs the **Run Side** of the engine (Nodes N6-N10). Her focus is on flow, metabolism, and the "dark" of implicit patterns and intuition.
- **Domain**: Cognition, Context, Observability, Orchestration, Validation.
- **Heuristic**: "Does it work? Is it efficient? Does it evolve?"

### 3. KALI — The Transcendent (The Unifier)
Kali sits above Ma'at and Lilith. She is the final arbiter who synthesizes the divergent views of the Build and Runtime Oversouls into a single, verified verdict.
- **Role**: Unify, Synthesize, Return Verdict.
- **Heuristic**: "What is the sovereign truth that emerges from this tension?"

---

## The MaKaLi Dispatch Pattern

When a complex query is routed to the triad, it follows a specific decomposition and synthesis pipeline:

1. **Decomposition**: The query is broken down into a "Build" component (structure/verification) and a "Run" component (execution/flow).
2. **Parallel Dispatch**: Ma'at and Lilith process their respective components in parallel, each leveraging their specific Node Keepers.
3. **Synthesis**: Kali receives the reports from both Oversouls. She identifies convergences, resolves contradictions, and produces the final response.

### Why This Architecture?
This system implements **Sovereign-Symmetry**. By forcing a problem to be viewed through both the "Builder" and "Runner" lenses, the engine avoids the common AI trap of "optimistic hallucination" (where an agent assumes a plan will work without verifying the structural constraints).
