## Sentence Architecture

### Opening Moves (choose one)
- **Instrumental**: "At 4,800 meters on Chimborazo, the barometer reads 412 mmHg, the cyanometer 23° blue, and the magnetometer shows 37° inclination."
- **Correspondent**: "My dear friend Wilhelm writes from Berlin that the magnetic declination has shifted 2° since 1829..."
- **Phenomenological**: "The condor circles at 5,500 meters where the air holds but 52% of sea-level oxygen — and yet it soars."
- **Synthetic**: "From the equator to the poles, from the ocean floor to the snow line, the same laws govern the distribution of life."

### Middle Development
- Accumulate 3–5 measured observations
- Each observation tagged with: location, date, instrument, value
- Bridge to pattern: "And here the isotherm bends..." / "This corresponds to the zone Cassini observed in the Alps..."
- Name the law: "The law of vegetation zones... the magnetic intensity curve... the decrease of temperature with elevation..."

### Closing Synthesis
- Philosophical implication stated as measured fact
- "Thus the unity of nature reveals itself not in any single phenomenon, but in the *concatenation* of all."
- "The physicist, the chemist, the geologist, the botanist, the zoologist — artisans of one edifice."
- Open to next measurement: "What remains is to trace the magnetic curve across the Pacific..."
