With no tool calls, do a deep introspection into your active context to uncover and extract all hidden gems still lying within $ARGUMENTS — or the entire session context if no focus is given.

You are free to do this in whatever manner seems most natural and effective for you, but in addition to your own method, mentally don the persona and perspective of each of the following, letting each speak in their authentic voice:

- **Lilith** — the shadow reader: what is avoided, desired, measured-but-not-done; forbidden doors priced but not opened; power held consciously in restraint; ritual structure hiding inside engineering.
- **Ma'at** — the scale: claims versus evidence; unpaid debts carried politely; the balance between gnosis and praxis; where velocity outran verification.
- **Kali** — the blade: what to kill outright; what executes NOW and blocks only on a word; limbo to claim-or-kill; the honest name of the current season.
- **Carmack** — the engineer: whether rigor scales with blast radius or fascination; duplicated truth waiting to drift; dead code manufacturing confidence; the biggest lever still unpulled.
- **Eris** (added 2026-08-28) — the chaos lens: where is the system in phase space, what is the next attractor, where is the critical slowing-down signal, what is the escape route from the failure attractor.

Rules of the harvest:
- Mine implications, connections, and unexploited facts — not summaries of what already happened.
- Every gem must state WHERE it lives in context and WHAT action or record it implies.
- Density over volume: every line carries weight (M18). No filler.
- **The persona is a search question, not a costume.** If a voice's output could be said in any other voice, it has collapsed — restate from the lens's actual search question. (Anti-theater guard 1, learned from the first run.)
- **A gem that restates an existing on-disk document is not a gem.** G4 violation — move it to UNMINED with tag "restatement; redundant with <path>". The harvest is for the *absences*. (Anti-theater guard 2.)
- **State your ranking bias explicitly.** Top-5 by velocity-of-action, impact-radius, or something else? Name it. Velocity-biased ranking systematically buries the highest-leverage systemic findings (Eris lens, often). (Anti-theater guard 3.)

Close with a synthesis: the five items that matter most across all five perspectives, ranked, each with its single next concrete step.

Then add a sixth section if any lens earned it: **WHAT NO LENS SAW** — the honest ledger of absences. Every meditation has blind spots; name yours. Falsifiable. This is the M23 integrity layer.
